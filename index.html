A small place for a long chat.

Koupa is a neighbourhood café in Nadapuram. Drop in for a quick roll on the way somewhere, or come by in the evening with friends for burgers, loaded fries and a cold shake.

Everything is made to order behind the counter, so it reaches you hot. Pull up a stool, or take it with you.
